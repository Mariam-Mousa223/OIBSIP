Honoring Dr. Ahmed Zewail

Source: Portrait of Dr. Ahmed Zewail - Chemist and Nobel Laureate

Biography & Academic Journey

Dr. Ahmed Hassan Zewail was born on February 26, 1946, in Damanhur, Egypt, and raised in Desouk. He showed an early passion for natural sciences, which led him to join the Faculty of Science at Alexandria University, earning his Bachelor of Science degree with distinction and first-class honors in 1967. He then worked as a demonstrator and earned his Master's degree in Light Science.

Zewail traveled to the United States on a scientific journey to pursue his higher studies, obtaining his Ph.D. from the University of Pennsylvania in 1974. Afterwards, he joined the California Institute of Technology (Caltech), climbing the academic ranks until he became a Professor of Chemistry and Physics, holding the highest scientific chair at the institute.

He developed a ultra-fast laser imaging system for "Femtochemistry", enabling the observation of molecular movement during their formation and chemical reactions. This breakthrough opened entirely new horizons in physics, chemistry, biology, and medicine, allowing humanity for the first time to witness chemical reactions in real-time history.

His illustrious career culminated in winning the Nobel Prize in Chemistry in 1999, becoming the first Egyptian and Arab scientist to win prestigious award in a scientific field. His contributions didn't stop at research; he established "Zewail City of Science and Technology" in Egypt to be a beacon of scientific excellence, nurturing new generations of researchers.

"Technology is no longer a luxury, but the foundation for the future of nations. Education is the true weapon to overcome all challenges."

— Dr. Ahmed Zewail
