# Dr. Ahmed Zewail Tribute Page 🏛️

A respectful, elegant, and responsive tribute web page dedicated to honoring the legacy, academic journey, and historical milestones of Nobel Laureate Dr. Ahmed Zewail.

---

## 🚀 Overview & Concept

Created as part of the **Immortals Platform** concept, this tribute page serves as an educational showcase highlighting Dr. Ahmed Zewail's contribution to Femtochemistry and science. Designed with a dark UI, subtle neon accents, structured biography text, inspirational quotes, and an interactive milestone timeline.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **Header Navigation:** Features the platform logo ("Immortals Platform") and title banner.
- **Featured Portrait:** High-resolution photo card with glowing borders and accurate source captioning.
- **Biography & Academic Journey:** Detailed chronological narrative covering Dr. Zewail's early life in Egypt, studies at Alexandria University, Ph.D. at Penn, and tenure at Caltech.
- **Inspirational Quote Block:** Highlighted blockquote emphasizing the importance of technology and education.
- **Key Milestones Timeline:** Responsive card grid highlighting important career years:
  - **1967:** Graduation & Master's Degree.
  - **1989:** King Faisal International Prize in Science.
  - **1999:** Nobel Prize in Chemistry (Femtochemistry).
  - **2011:** Founding Zewail City of Science and Technology.
- **Footer:** Clean copyright and purpose statement.

---

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- **HTML5:** Semantic document structure, article blocks, figures, and quote elements.
- **CSS3:** Custom dark theme styling, Flexbox/Grid layout, glowing border cards, and typography controls.
